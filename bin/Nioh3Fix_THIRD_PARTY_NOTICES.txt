## nioh3fps

Nioh3Fix's `src/vfr/` is a port of nioh3fps (upstream commit `014e50d4f5028aa5b5a4e6137d44c01be60c4be3`). Changes made for
Nioh3Fix: it is compiled into this DLL, so its own diagnostics (trace recorder, probes,
crash handler, extra heartbeat sources, CSV/log writer) were removed. It exports its
update-thread state and the native pacer's timing to the rest of Nioh3Fix. A hybrid controller
can switch variable timestep off below a frame-rate threshold, and the online gate has an opt-in.
